Principal Investigator

Yongtae Lee, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor

Yongtae Lee designs analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits for sensor interfaces. His doctoral work produced silicon-verified resistor-to-digital converters and precision temperature sensors published in IEEE JSSC, SSC-L, and TCAS-II, and presented at ISSCC, the VLSI Symposium, CICC, and ESSCIRC. Before joining Kongju National University he designed sensor circuits at Samsung Electronics System LSI. At ICSIL he is extending that line of work toward integrated front-ends for battery impedance spectroscopy.

  • 2025 –Assistant Professor, Division of Electrical, Electronic and Control Engineering, Kongju National University
  • 2023 – 2025Staff Engineer, Sensor Design Team, System LSI Business, Samsung Electronics

Students

We are recruiting.

Early members shape the lab.

ICSIL is a new laboratory, which means the first students take first-author responsibility on the work they lead. If you want to understand a measurement system end to end — from the transistor to the instrument on the bench — this is a good place to do it.

M.S. / Ph.D. Students Analog IC design, mixed-signal readout circuits, battery instrumentation.
Undergraduate Researchers PCB design, MATLAB/Simulink modelling, FPGA-based measurement setups.
What we look for No prior IC design experience required. A solid grasp of circuit theory and genuine curiosity matter more.

Interested students are welcome to email a short note describing their background and what they would like to work on — ytlee@kongju.ac.kr
